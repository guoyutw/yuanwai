# 園外 Yuanwai 0.1

一個公開打造中的 AI 原生一人公司實驗。

園外從我過去實際經營餐飲、外燴的經驗開始。
我不先決定它最後會是一間外燴公司、AI Agent、媒合平台，還是 SaaS。
先把真實需求做成能運作的系統，再用實際使用、失敗與回饋決定下一步。

這個 Repository 會公開記錄：

- 現在正在測什麼
- 做了什麼
- 得到什麼證據
- 哪些假設錯了
- 為什麼改方向
- 下一步要做什麼

不是成功後才整理的案例，而是公司正在形成的過程本身。

Yuanwai is a public build-in-public experiment in building an AI-native solo company.
It starts from my real experience running a restaurant and handling catering work.
I am intentionally not deciding upfront whether Yuanwai will eventually become a catering business, an AI agent, a marketplace, a SaaS product, or something else.
The approach is simple: build around real problems first, collect real-world evidence, and let that evidence decide what the company becomes.

This repository records the process as it happens:

- what I am testing
- what I built
- what evidence I found
- what failed
- why the direction changed
- what comes next

This is not a polished case study written after success.
It is the operating record of the company while it is being built.

## How this repository learns

`hypothesis → build/do → real-world evidence → review → decision/pivot → durable writeback → NEXT ACTION`

Knowledge moves through `evidence → candidate → canonical`. A one-off observation is not automatically a rule, and superseded strategies remain visible.
