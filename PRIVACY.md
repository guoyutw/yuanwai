# Privacy boundary

Yuanwai is public-by-default for non-sensitive company thinking and work artifacts.

Never commit:

- customer names, contact details, identifiers, or other PII;
- raw private LINE conversations;
- credentials, access tokens, API keys, or secret configuration;
- private supplier pricing;
- payment, financial, or other sensitive business data.

Public examples must be synthetic or de-identified. When in doubt, keep the material out of the public repository and record only the decision or lesson needed for the operating history.
