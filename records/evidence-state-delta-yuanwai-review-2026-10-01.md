# Evidence Review: Yuanwai Signals from Historical State Deltas

**Status: public-safe synthesis of historical owner evidence.**

**Reviewed scope:** ChatGPT State Delta raw reviews covering 2026-09-17 through 2026-09-29, compared against the current Yuanwai repository state on 2026-10-01.

This record does not promote every historical idea. Its purpose is to identify which reusable Yuanwai learnings were already captured, which were still missing, and which older ideas should remain non-canonical.

## Already represented in the repository

The following historical signals are already substantially represented by existing Yuanwai records and do not need a new product-direction decision:

- LINE Official Account as a plausible customer entry for the customer ↔ Yuanwai ↔ vendor flow.
- Human-in-the-loop handling, with AI assisting organization, drafting, forwarding, and recording but not making vendor business commitments.
- Progressive qualification rather than forcing a complete RFQ at first contact.
- The recent-customer cohort remaining consistent with progressive qualification and surfacing later-stage logistics, invoice/administrative, equipment/setup, and menu-revision needs.
- Vendor economics as an open hypothesis: Yuanwai may eventually need to distinguish merely acceptable work from work that is worthwhile for the vendor, including order size, labor, transport, setup, capacity, and operational complexity.

## Missing but reusable owner evidence

### Blossom as a real-world experiment environment

On 2026-09-23, the owner explicitly narrowed the purpose of Blossom's remaining operating period: do not keep expanding the restaurant itself; use the still-live business as a low-cost environment for experiments whose learning can carry forward.

For Yuanwai, the highest-value use of that environment was real catering workflow testing.

This is operational context, not evidence that Blossom's economics or workflow generalize to other vendors.

### First live Yuanwai case should come from a real Blossom inquiry

On 2026-09-29, the owner narrowed the Yuanwai MVP from abstract platform design to a concrete live path:

`real Blossom catering inquiry → customer enters Yuanwai LINE → run the workflow once`

At end of day, the intake/filter had not yet been implemented and no live case had completed.

This directly refines how to obtain the current five live qualification cases without changing the qualification candidate itself.

## Historical ideas intentionally not promoted

The following appeared in earlier exploration but should remain non-canonical unless new evidence revives them:

- Taoyuan-only market scope.
- A transaction-fee model or the early approximately 6% commission reference.
- A decision that Yuanwai is a marketplace, lead-generation platform, SaaS product, or autonomous agent.
- The separate Blossom LINE/n8n prototype as a requirement for the current Yuanwai MVP.
- Any claim that Yuanwai has validated independent customer acquisition, organic distribution, or multi-vendor matching.

## Resulting writeback

The reusable missing evidence above supports a bounded operating decision: use Blossom's remaining 2026 operating period as the initial Yuanwai live sandbox, while keeping the existing progressive-qualification candidate and five-case clear condition intact.
