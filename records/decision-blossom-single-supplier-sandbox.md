# Decision: Blossom Single-Supplier Sandbox

**Status: current owner-approved experiment setup for 2026-10–12. Not a canonical final business model.**

## Decision

During the remaining 2026 operating period of Blossom Bistro, Yuanwai will use Blossom as the **sole supplier** for the initial live qualification experiment.

New catering inquiries that would otherwise go directly to Blossom should first be routed through Yuanwai's LINE customer entry, so Yuanwai acts as the qualification / coordination layer before Blossom continues with pricing and fulfillment.

This narrows the execution environment for the current MVP. It does not decide that Yuanwai is a single-vendor business, marketplace, lead-generation company, SaaS product, or autonomous agent.

## Initial flow

`Blossom-originated catering inquiry → Yuanwai LINE → progressive qualification → supplier-ready brief → human confirmation → Blossom pricing / proposal / fulfillment`

Yuanwai owns the customer-side qualification and coordination experiment.

Blossom remains responsible for supplier-side business commitments such as availability, price, menu, and fulfillment.

## What this can validate

This sandbox can provide live evidence about:

- whether a real customer will continue the inquiry through Yuanwai;
- whether progressive qualification preserves known information and avoids redundant questions;
- whether the supplier-ready brief reduces vendor re-asking;
- where observable friction or abandonment appears before first price;
- whether the current qualification candidate survives real use.

## What this does not validate

This setup does **not** by itself validate:

- independent Yuanwai customer acquisition or organic distribution;
- multi-vendor matching or ranking;
- external supplier onboarding;
- vendor economics generalization beyond Blossom;
- autonomous pricing, negotiation, or fulfillment commitments;
- the final Yuanwai business model.

The existing five-case qualification experiment and its CLEAR CONDITION remain the acceptance boundary.
