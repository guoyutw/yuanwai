# Working Hypothesis: 客戶 ↔ 園外 AI 中間層 ↔ 店家

**Status: Working hypothesis — not canonical.**

Yuanwai may become an AI-mediated middle layer between catering customers and participating vendors. This is a direction to test, not a decision that Yuanwai is already a marketplace, matching platform, or autonomous agent.

## What this could look like

- Customer demand may enter through LINE OA and be structured into a usable request.
- Yuanwai may use known vendor information to surface plausible vendors.
- Early tests may use existing relationships with catering vendors, but this record does not commit to a vendor set or a matching model.
- 哲瑜 remains in the loop and may manually decide whom to contact or refer.
- AI may assist with organizing requests, forwarding information, drafting messages, and recording decisions.
- If a customer asks to change an important business detail, such as changing tea to milk tea, AI must ask the vendor and reply based on the vendor's decision. AI does not make the business commitment itself.

## Evidence we still need

Important customer-side and vendor-side messages should eventually be recoverable from Yuanwai-owned data so the interaction can be reviewed and learned from.

The next implementation milestone remains the existing minimal LINE foundation: one test inbound message, one outbound reply, and recovery of both records. This hypothesis does not change that NEXT ACTION.

## Explicit non-decisions

This record does not define:

- a vendor-matching algorithm, ranking, or distance policy;
- a marketplace, commission model, or full multi-vendor platform;
- autonomous vendor negotiation or customer service;
- a database schema, framework, hosting provider, or agent architecture;
- canonical product direction.

Promotion from hypothesis to candidate or canonical direction requires later real-world evidence and review.
